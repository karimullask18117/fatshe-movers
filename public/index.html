What we move

Four kinds of move

House shifting

1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK and larger. Furniture is dismantled and refitted, kitchen appliances are wrapped, and boxes are labelled by room.

Office shifting

Desks, workstations, files and IT equipment, planned around your working days. Tell us the headcount and we size the move.

Car transport

Hatchback, sedan and SUV to cities across India. Cars travel on a separate vehicle from your household goods.

Delicate items

Smart TVs, glass and sofas are packed with foam and moving blankets, and loaded so they are not stacked under heavy goods.

From first message to unloading

How a booking works

  1. Share your details

    Fill the form, call, or message us on WhatsApp with your from and to addresses and moving date.

  2. Survey

    We visit your home, or you show us each room on a WhatsApp video call. We write up the checklist of items.

  3. Written quote

    You get an itemised estimate: packing, loading, transport and unloading listed separately.

  4. Book and move

    Confirm the booking amount with customer care. On the day we pack, load, transport and unload at the new address.

Before you book

Common questions

Is the first price I hear the final price?

The rates on this page are a guide. After the survey you get a written estimate. If your load and access match what we surveyed, the amount stays the same on moving day.

Do I need to be at home for a survey?

No. If you are in Bangalore we can visit, and if you are elsewhere or short on time, a WhatsApp video call works. Show us the rooms, wardrobes and any large or delicate items.

How do I book?

Accept the written quote, then confirm the booking amount with our customer care. We fix the date and crew after that.
